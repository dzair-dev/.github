## About

We're building a space for Algeria's technology community to connect, share ideas, showcase projects, and grow together.

Whether you're a developer, designer, product manager, founder, student, or technology enthusiast, dzair.dev is for you.

## What we're building

- **Connect** — Bring people from across Algeria's tech ecosystem together.
- **Showcase** — Give local projects, ideas, and talent more visibility.
- **Collaborate** — Help people find teammates and build together.
- **Grow** — Encourage knowledge sharing and new opportunities.
