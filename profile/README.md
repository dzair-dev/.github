## About

Across Algeria, talented people are building ideas, launching projects, exploring new technologies, and shaping the future.

Yet, many of them don't have enough opportunities to discover each other, showcase their work, exchange knowledge, or collaborate.

**dzair.dev aims to change that.**

We're building a space where Algeria's technology ecosystem can become more visible, connected, and collaborative.

Whether you're a developer, designer, product manager, founder, student, or simply passionate about technology, there's a place for you here.
